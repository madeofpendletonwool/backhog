# Attributions

Backhog's interface is built from other people's art. This file records who
made what, under which terms, and what Backhog did to it.

The upstream packs themselves are **not** in this repository. `icon-packs/`
is gitignored; what ships in `web/public/assets/` is the derived output of
`scripts/build-assets.py`, which recolours the nine-slice frames into
Backhog's own console-plastic palette and copies the rest through unchanged.
Anyone rebuilding the assets needs to obtain the packs from the links below
first (all four are free downloads, no account required).

---

## Pixel art

### Pixel UI Pack (750 assets)
**Kenney** · <https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-ui-pack-750-assets> · **CC0**

Every framed surface in the app: the panel behind cards and dialogs, the
sunken field text sits in, the coin-gold and danger-red buttons, and the
chip pills used for status badges and filters. Backhog ships adaptations —
`panel.png`, `field.png`, `button-gold.png` and the rest are recoloured into
the theme's own plastic and the constant coin-gold/danger-red ramps. The
recolour maps live in `scripts/build-assets.py`.

### Pixel Arcade Stick and Buttons Expansion
**optimusgnu** · <https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-arcade-stick-and-buttons-expansion> · **CC0**

The joystick and arcade-button sprites: the sidebar wordmark, the mobile
header mark, the favicon. Shipped unmodified as `web/public/assets/sprites/arcade.png` —
its own DB16 palette is the point, so nothing is recoloured.

### Parallax City — Night (4 Colors)
**fisherg** · <https://opengameart.org/content/parallax-city-night-4-colors> · **CC0**

The **arcade** theme's backdrop — a Game Boy–resolution (160×144) neon
street, six layers. Its own hue (measured at build time) is what gives the
arcade theme its violet-magenta cast.

### Forest Parallax
**robotrage** · <https://opengameart.org/content/forest-parallax> · **CC0**

The **forest** theme's backdrop — three silhouette layers of a night
tree-line.

### Seamless Parallax Cave Background
**jonathanpalmergd** · <https://opengameart.org/content/seamless-parallax-cave-background> · **CC0**

The **cavern** theme's backdrop — four painted layers of a lantern-lit dig.

### Mountain at Dusk Background
**ansimuz** · <https://opengameart.org/content/mountain-at-dusk-background> · **CC0**

The **dusk** theme's backdrop — five silhouette layers of a mountain ridge
at last light.

All four scene packs ship unmodified in `web/public/assets/scenes/<theme>/`,
numbered front-to-back; `scripts/build-assets.py` reads each one's own
pixels to derive that theme's chrome ramp and sky colour (see
`docs/DESIGN.md`).

**Lorc**, **Delapouite**, **guard13007**, and the **badges** set ·
<https://game-icons.net> · **CC BY 3.0**

affordance (search, settings, close, trash, grab handles, chevrons…) and
the small nouns (trophy, hourglass, dice, gamepad, calendar…) that would
mush as pixel art at 16px. This replaced lucide-react entirely.

## Fonts

Self-hosted in `web/public/assets/fonts/` — nothing loads from Google
Fonts or any other CDN. Fetched by `scripts/fetch-fonts.py`.

| Font | By | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Silkscreen](https://github.com/googlefonts/silkscreen) | Jason Kottke | OFL 1.1 | Wordmark, nav labels, buttons, stat numbers — the console/label voice |

Prose and body chrome stay on the system UI font stack — no bytes, no
CDN, present on every machine already.
